Flexible schedule

The four-hour plan

Times are guides, not deadlines. Photography and an appealing food stop are valid reasons to adjust the route.

12:31–1:00

Arrive, transfer luggage and collect bicycles

Walk from the station to 客尊, confirm bicycle type, brakes, lights, locks and any La Brume discount. Ask the shop to mark the recommended return path.

1:00–1:35

Quick lunch in town

池上豆之間 is the most distinctive option if service is quick. A takeaway Chishang bento is the safest choice when timing is tight.

1:35–2:15

Dapo Pond · 大坡池

Enjoy a short lakeside section rather than attempting a full slow circuit. If delayed, trim this stop first.

2:15–3:30

Canal-side route → Brown Boulevard and 金城武樹

Use the 池上圳 cycling route where practical. This is the countryside priority: rice fields, mountain views and relaxed photography.

3:30–4:00

Paradise Road · 天堂路

Continue only while the family is comfortable and daylight remains good. These are working farm roads, so watch for agricultural vehicles.

4:00–4:45

Ride back towards town

Turn back by 4:00pm even if one photo stop is missed. Avoid experimenting with unfamiliar side roads late in the ride.

By 5:00

Return bicycles to 客尊

Collect any receipt or belongings, then travel to La Brume for check-in and a restful evening.

Why the canal-side route?

Chishang Township recommends the 池上圳 route between Dapo Pond and Brown Boulevard. The alternative beside East-9 is narrow, cannot comfortably accommodate two slow vehicles passing, and may run alongside gravel trucks.

Food stops

Do not sacrifice Brown Boulevard for a queue. Pick one proper lunch or one quick takeaway, then start riding.

Best fit

池上豆之間

Soybean dishes, tofu-skin rolls, soy milk and tofu pudding. Listed 08:00–17:00 at 中山路通水巷12號, about five minutes’ walk from the station.

If the day slips

Protect the best part

Train delay, luggage handover or lunch can consume the first hour. Shorten Dapo Pond and use a takeaway lunch before cutting Brown Boulevard. The countryside ride is the reason for this afternoon; every other stop is adjustable.
