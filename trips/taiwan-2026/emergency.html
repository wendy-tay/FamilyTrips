General area ↗

During and immediately after

Earthquake

1

Drop

Get down before the shaking knocks you over. Do not run outside.

2

Cover

Protect your head and neck under a sturdy table. Stay away from windows and tall furniture.

3

Hold on

Hold the shelter until the shaking stops. If there is no table, crouch beside an interior wall.

After the shaking

  • Expect aftershocks.
  • Put on shoes before walking around broken glass.
  • Use stairs—never lifts.
  • If you smell gas, do not use flames or electrical switches.
  • Follow hotel staff and official alerts.

If driving

  • Slow down and pull over safely, away from bridges, tunnels, slopes and power lines.
  • Stay inside until the shaking stops.
  • Check road closures before continuing; beware of rockfall in Hualien, Taitung and Alishan.

If outdoors

  • Move away from buildings, glass, signs and power lines.
  • In the mountains, move away from steep slopes and cliff faces.
  • At the coast, follow the tsunami rule below as soon as shaking stops.

Especially Hualien, Qixingtan and Taitung

Tsunami

5 sec siren5 sec silence5 sec siren

Then a voice announcement may repeat: 海嘯警報！請所有民眾迅速往高處疏散！ If there is no voice system, the 5-second siren / 5-second silence pattern repeats.

All-clear: one continuous 90-second siren. Do not return before authorities give the all-clear.

Natural warning = leave now

If the earthquake is strong enough to make standing difficult, or the shaking continues for roughly a minute, do not wait for a phone alert or siren.

  1. Move inland and uphill on foot.
  2. Do not stop for luggage or the car.
  3. If no hill is reachable, enter a sturdy reinforced building and go to at least the third floor.
  4. Stay away: later waves can be larger than the first.

If separated

Family self-help plan

01

Safety first

During a coastal emergency, each person evacuates immediately. Do not return to search at the shoreline.

02

Send one message

From a safe place, send the family group your location, condition and who is with you. Text may work when calls do not.

03

Show the address

If lost, open this page for hotel stays. In Taipei, use the privately saved Airbnb emergency card.

04

Keep it offline

Screenshot the current address each morning. Keep parents’ numbers and one printed card inside the phone case.
