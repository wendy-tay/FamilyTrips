Taipei · 2–6 December

Apartment neighbourhood and central Taipei

Start with the apartment’s Zhongshan neighbourhood. Cross-city queues are optional, especially when the family would rather shop.

Near the Zhongshan apartment

Convenient for an unplanned dinner or supper without turning the evening into another excursion.

Priority

遼寧街夜市

Dinner / supper · street-food cluster

A compact food-focused night market for seafood stir-fries, oyster omelette, goose, sesame-oil dishes, sweet soup and tofu pudding. More practical than travelling across Taipei for a larger market.

General market hours reported around 18:00–24:00; individual stalls vary.

Eat if nearby

梁記嘉義雞肉飯

Lunch / early dinner · chicken rice

Try chicken rice or chicken-and-braised-pork rice with a soft fried egg and side dishes. A sensible weekday meal around Songjiang Nanjing.

Mon–Fri 10:00–14:30 and 16:30–20:00; closed weekends. Office-hour peaks can mean a queue or shared table.

Taipei Main Station, Q Square and Huashan

Useful around the teens’ Q Square visit or a central-Taipei morning.

Optional

阜杭豆漿

Breakfast · Huashan Market

Thick baked flatbread with egg, salty soy milk and fried dough. Famous, but the queue can exceed an hour—skip without regret if it steals family time.

Tue–Sun 05:30–12:30; Monday closed; may sell out early.

Eat if nearby

寧夏夜市

Dinner / grazing · Datong District

A concentrated traditional-snack market that pairs reasonably with the Taipei Main and Datong area. Share small portions rather than chasing every famous stall.

Most stalls operate from late afternoon into the evening; each vendor keeps its own hours.

Hualien · 22–24 November

Station, Kadda and the harbour

Protect the Qingshui Cliff departure on arrival day: a quick station-area lunch is more valuable than a long queue.

Hualien TRA Station · quick arrival lunch

Use these before collecting the car. 車頭虱目魚 is deliberately not listed as lunch because reliable sources place its opening in the late afternoon.

Priority

職饗雞湯小卷米粉

Lunch · chicken broth and fresh squid rice noodles

A local sit-down choice about 5–10 minutes from the station. Use it only if Train 270 is punctual and the queue is short.

Sunday lunch has been listed around 11:00–14:00; recheck on arrival.

Optional

花蓮車站餐飲區

Fast fallback · takeaway

Choose a station eatery, convenience-store meal or takeaway counter if the train or car collection runs late. This is the best way to protect the afternoon cliff visit.

Hours vary by tenant.

Near Kadda Hotel and Hualien city

Low-effort family dinner choices after driving or the coastal day.

Priority

育樂新村9號美食熱炒

Dinner · shared stir-fry dishes

A convenient casual family meal near Kadda: order rice, vegetables, seafood and meat dishes to share.

Published hours vary. Call 03-833-0115 that afternoon before walking over.

Eat if nearby

山本屋

Dinner · seafood and 熱炒

Seafood, sashimi, fried rice, noodles and shared dishes. Best when the family wants a broader menu and does not mind a short ride.

Dinner is commonly listed from about 17:30; Tuesday is the usual rest day. Recheck same-day.

Optional

阿嬤麵店

Early dinner · comforting noodles

A simple fallback when everyone is tired. Go early because it is commonly reported to close around 19:00.

Same-day opening information should be checked.

Optional

車頭虱目魚專賣店

Supper · milkfish soup and braised-pork rice

A station-area late meal rather than an arrival lunch. Ask for boneless milkfish belly soup, while still checking carefully for bones.

Sources consistently indicate late-afternoon opening, but differ between about 16:30 and 17:30; often runs into the night.

Chishang · 24 November

Soybean food beside the cycling plan

Choose one quick lunch before the countryside ride. Brown Boulevard remains the priority; do not wait in a long food queue.

Station and town centre

These stops are already connected to the dedicated cycling page.

Priority

池上豆之間

Lunch · soybean dishes

Tofu-skin rolls, soy milk, tofu pudding and other soybean dishes near the station. Best distinctive lunch if service is quick.

Listed 08:00–17:00; about five minutes from the station.

Eat if nearby

大池豆皮店

Early lunch / snack · fresh tofu skin

Freshly made tofu skin and pan-fried tofu rolls. Use only if collection is fast and the shop has stock.

Tuesday is reported open from early morning until about 14:00 or sold out; Wednesday is the usual closure. Hours vary across listings.

Optional

福原豆腐店

Tofu snack · only if visibly open

Fried tofu and unusual tofu-pudding flavours, including wasabi soy. Do not route around it on this trip.

Recent sources conflict; several report Tuesday closure. Treat it as an accidental bonus only.

Optional

多力米故事館

Rice-themed town stop

The museum and adjacent restaurant are useful if the family wants a rice-focused meal before cycling.

Museum 08:00–19:00; adjacent restaurant reported 11:00–19:00 and closed Wed–Thu.

Optional

池上便當

Fast fallback · takeaway

The safest choice if the train is late or a shop has a queue. Eat before the countryside section rather than carrying an awkward meal on the bicycles.

Choose an open shop near the station on arrival.

Open the cycling plan and route map

水也上居 is excluded from the working Tuesday choices because Tuesday is a reported closure day.

Taitung · 25–27 November

City grazing, supper and the northern coast

The city options suit takeaway and shared tasting. Order back to Machiya when everyone is tired; food does not need another itinerary.

Machiya Posong, Zhengqi Road and the old city

Most of the useful city choices cluster around the walkable centre.

PriorityTakeaway + delivery

藍蜻蜓速食專賣店

Dinner / supper · Taiwanese fried chicken

Fried chicken, chicken pieces, fries and burgers. Good for bringing back to the villa; Uber Eats delivery has been listed.

Reported 10:30–23:00, Monday closed. Expect a queue at popular times.

Priority

榕樹下米苔目

Lunch / dinner · rice noodles and braised sides

Order soup or dry rice noodles, then add fish balls, tofu, braised egg or other small plates. Fast-moving but very popular.

The official site lists 09:30–15:00 and 17:00–20:00, Wednesday closed; address listings conflict between the old and current premises, so use the linked live map pin.

Eat if nearby

林家臭豆腐

Afternoon / dinner snack · crispy tofu

Twice-fried tofu with sauce, pickled cabbage and basil. Share a plate during a Zhengqi Road grazing walk.

Mon–Fri 14:00–20:00; weekends from 12:00; may close when sold out. Queues are common.

OptionalSupper + delivery

阿鋐炸雞

Takeaway supper · fried chicken alternative

A second well-known local fried-chicken choice. The Zhengqi branch suits a late takeaway; a separate Xinsheng branch appears on delivery apps.

Zhengqi branch commonly reported 10:45–23:00, Tuesday closed. Delivery coverage and hours depend on the app and address.

OptionalSweet supper

寶桑豆花

Dessert / evening snack · traditional tofu pudding

A light finish after fried food or the night market. Go before the favourite toppings sell out.

Recent listings place it around 13:00–21:10 or 14:00–22:00; recheck the live map on the day.

Eat if nearby

台東觀光夜市

Thursday dinner · street-food grazing

Use the market only if the family wants variety after returning from the coast. Look for guava or 甘草芭樂 when the fruit appears fresh.

The working plan is Thursday evening; individual vendors vary and weather affects attendance.

Fugang and the northern-coast day

Eat where the route naturally passes the harbour instead of returning to the city for a famous shop.

Eat if nearby

富岡漁港活海產餐廳

Lunch · shared seafood

A named harbour option for the Xiaoyeliu–Fugang route. Confirm price and portion before ordering; choose a few shared dishes rather than a large set.

Location is verified, but dependable current opening hours were not found. Check the live listing or ask locally that morning.

Tainan · 27–29 November

Fun Simon, Bao’an Road and Anping

Small portions are the strategy. Share one bowl or plate at each appealing stop and leave room for the next discovery.

Near Fun Simon · Bao’an Road and West Central District

The highest-value cluster: distinctive Tainan dishes within a short walk of the accommodation.

Priority

鼎富發豬油拌飯

Dinner tasting · pork-lard rice

Share fragrant pork-lard rice with egg yolk and crispy shallots. Treat it as the first taste, not a full dinner for four.

Use the linked live pin for current hours; queues can form at peak meal times.

Priority

集品蝦仁飯

Lunch / dinner tasting · shrimp rice

A small bowl of rice cooked with bonito and shrimp broth, topped with stir-fried shrimp and duck egg.

Reported around 09:30–21:00; recheck on the day.

Priority

保安路米糕

Lunch / dinner tasting · Tainan rice cake

A compact serving of sticky rice with savoury toppings. Pair with soup and share if continuing a food walk.

Reported 10:00–22:00, Wednesday closed; confirm same-day.

Priority

阿堂鹹粥

Breakfast · milkfish and oyster porridge

A distinctly Tainan soup-style rice breakfast with fish and seafood. Go early or use another breakfast if the queue undermines the lazy morning.

Reported 05:00–12:30, Tuesday closed. Weekend queues can be long after about 08:00.

Eat if nearby

六千牛肉湯

Very early breakfast · fresh beef soup

A famous dawn option extremely close to the accommodation, but not worth forcing another early alarm unless the family actively wants it.

Sells out early and commonly has a queue. Check the current opening day and arrive only if everyone agrees.

Optional

窄門咖啡館

Café experience · Confucius Temple area

The narrow entrance and hidden historic-house setting make this an activity as much as a café. Keep it for the children only if the quirky entrance appeals.

Check the live listing before walking over.

Anping and the Sicao day

Choose lunch only if it fits the sightseeing flow; the day does not need a restaurant detour.

Eat if nearby

周氏蝦捲

Lunch / snack · shrimp rolls

An easy family choice around Anping: shrimp rolls, noodles, rice and soup. Convenient rather than compulsory.

Multiple branches exist. Use the linked Anping pin and verify the branch hours that morning.

Optional

武聖夜市

Saturday dinner · snacks and games

A local-feeling family market with traditional snacks and game stalls. Take a taxi and browse according to appetite.

The trip plan uses Saturday evening; outdoor operation can be affected by weather.

Family food rule

Convenience beats completion

Pick one priority when it naturally fits. If the queue is long, the shop is closed or the family is tired, choose the nearest appealing alternative. The trip is not a restaurant checklist.

Locations and hours were cross-checked against tourism authorities, official or ordering pages, and recent business listings. Conflicts are labelled on the relevant card. A Google Maps link identifies the intended business but same-day opening remains the shop’s decision.
