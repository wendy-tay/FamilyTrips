Pokémon, Pingu & Link Click

Top priority · confirmed for our dates

Pokémon Center POP-UP STORE

An official Pokémon Center pop-up selling original plush toys, cards, mascots and lifestyle goods. The event runs from 26 November to 13 December 2026, so it is open throughout our Tainan stay.

新光三越台南新天地 · 5F B區活動廣場 · immediately beside Fun Simon

Important: crowd control or timed-entry tickets may be used. Payment at the pop-up is by credit card or LINE Pay only; cash is not accepted. Stock and purchase limits may change.

Tainan cafés with an experience

Cat lover’s pick · reserve first

角落.有貓 Corner Cat Coffee · 台南赤崁樓店

A cat café inside a restored Japanese-era wooden residence near Chihkan Tower. The cats have their own glass corridor and interaction space, while the old-house setting, tatami rooms and garden make this more of an experience than an ordinary café.

忠義路二段233巷2號 · about five minutes’ walk from 赤崁樓 · official listing currently shows daily 10:30–19:00

Plan ahead: the café asks guests to reserve before visiting. A minimum spend of about NT$280 per person, a two-hour limit, 10% service charge and socks have been reported; reconfirm these conditions when booking.

Best unusual café

窄門咖啡 · Narrow Door Café

The attraction begins before the drinks: enter through a passage only about 38 cm wide between two buildings, climb the outdoor stairs and emerge inside a hidden second-floor old-house café overlooking the greenery near the Confucius Temple.

About 9 minutes’ walk from Fun Simon · worthwhile even if the children skip the temple

Optional backups

Old-house café shortlist

Tin Drum has a Japanese-era wooden house and garden; Tou Southwind combines a retro house with bakery and local crafts. L.M. Café is attractive but less distinctive, so keep it only if convenient.

Choose one café experience rather than trying to collect them all

How to choose: reserve 角落.有貓 if the family wants dedicated cat time near Chihkan Tower. Choose Narrow Door for a shorter, flexible café stop near the Confucius Temple. Toast Toast and Tomato Crepes remain non-priorities.
